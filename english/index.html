What is it?

The BTS SIO is a higher-education technology degree that trains professionals capable of meeting the IT needs of organizations.


Objective

It aims to prepare versatile technicians who can work throughout the entire life cycle of an IT system, from design to technical maintenance.

The program offers two options:


SLAM (Solutions Logicielles et Applications Métiers)

This option focuses on software development and the management of business applications. Students learn:


Application development: Programming languages such as JavaScript, C#, and modern software engineering methods.


Advanced databases: Interface design, business application creation, and IT project management.


Acquired skills: Database management, modeling, advanced querying, and agile project practices.


SISR (Solutions d'Infrastructure, Systèmes et Réseaux)

This option centers on the management of IT infrastructure.


System administration: Operating systems such as Windows Server and Linux plus the associated administration tasks.


Network management: Network architecture, communication protocols, and core network services.


Cybersecurity: Awareness of system and network security best practices.


Acquired skills: Server administration, IT fleet management, deployment of network solutions, and IT security.


After the first semester of the initial year, students must choose their option (SLAM or SISR).


Overview of the professional computer-based exams


Paper E5: Oral exam lasting 40 minutes in total.

  • 10-minute presentation of professional experience related to the chosen option.
  • 30-minute discussion with the jury about the professionalization experience and one or more projects detailed in the digital portfolio.

Paper E6: Practical oral exam lasting 40 minutes, with 1 hour 30 minutes of preparation. It is divided into two phases.

First phase: 30-minute preparation + 20-minute case study presentation.

  • Analyze and interpret the expression of needs.
  • Propose technical specifications and choose the most suitable tools.
  • Explain the planned approach to meet the expectations.

Second phase: 1-hour preparation + oral defense.

  • Present the solution and justify how it meets the requirements.
  • Answer questions about the operational solution, the tools used, and the methodology.
  • Carry out an individual implementation of the expected solution in the provided environment.

Paper E7: 4-hour written exam focused on cybersecurity.

  • Protect personal data.
  • Preserve the organization’s digital identity.
  • Secure equipment and user practices.
  • Guarantee the availability, integrity, and confidentiality of IT services and data.
  • Ensure the cybersecurity of an application solution and its development.

Technologies used at APRR:

PowerApps

PowerApps is a platform that allows the creation of custom applications without requiring programming skills. It is based on NoCode/LowCode using PowerFx, a formula language similar to Excel. It enables the creation of applications for mobile devices and web browsers.

PowerAutomate

Power Automate is a workflow service that automates tasks and processes between different applications and services. It allows the creation of automated workflows to synchronize files, collect data, send notifications, etc.

SharePoint

SharePoint facilitates collaborative and simple file sharing. It allows the creation of document libraries, lists, and websites to enhance team collaboration. It is often used by the Power Platform to store data and files associated with applications created using PowerApps and Power Automate.

Dataverse

Dataverse is a cloud database that allows storing and managing data for applications created with PowerApps and Power Automate. It offers advanced data management, security, and integration features with other Microsoft services. (This service is more expensive than SharePoint).
